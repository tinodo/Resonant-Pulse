Keep time.

A metronome and rhythm workstation for Windows. Set a tempo and play, or build your own rhythm in layers and practice it with a program that changes tempo as you go. Everything stays on your computer.

Features

A tempo to play along with, and the tools to build the rhythm you want to practice.

Play

A large dial with the tempo in the middle. Type an exact tempo, nudge it with -, TAP and +, or switch on Tap to start and tap the beat to begin at your own speed.

Time signatures

Choose from a sheet-music picker (2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 6/8, 12/8 and more) or build your own, with additive groups such as 3+2+2.

Beat strength and sound

Set the strength of every click with a row of beat bars, add a count-in, a subdivision or swing, and pick a sound.

Changes while it plays

Most changes are heard from the next beat, without stopping. A practice timer stops after a number of seconds, beats or bars.

Studio

Build a rhythm in lanes, each with its own pattern, sound, volume, pan and chance. Add a Euclidean rhythm, mute or solo a lane, and undo or redo. A change in Studio is heard after Stop, then Start.

Practice programs

Steps with tempo ramps, repeats, silent bars and a time signature of their own, which can repeat until you press Stop.

Train

Follow the program with a progress view and a chart of the planned tempo, and run a keyboard timing check that you can export as CSV.

Stage

A setlist for the stand, with notes, up next, a count-in for the next song, Lock editing and Always on top.

Library and export

Save projects, start from ready-made rhythms, and export WAV, MIDI, or one WAV per track in a ZIP.

Five languages

English, Dutch, German, French and Spanish, with light and dark themes, a Reduce motion switch and keyboard operation.

Microsoft Store

The easiest way to install. One click, and Microsoft Store keeps it up to date.

Resonant Pulse is being prepared for Microsoft Store and is not published there yet. Until it is, use the portable download below.

Portable ZIP

No installation needed.

  1. Open the latest release and download ResonantPulse-win-x64.zip, or ResonantPulse-win-arm64.zip for an ARM64 PC.
  2. Extract the whole ZIP anywhere and run ResonantPulse.exe. Do not copy the executable on its own.

The portable build is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may ask for confirmation the first time you run it. Each ZIP has a .sha256 file next to it: compare it with Get-FileHash .\ResonantPulse-win-x64.zip in PowerShell before you run it. The ARM64 build has not been tried on ARM64 hardware.

Private by design

Resonant Pulse works on your own computer. It does not use the microphone or the internet, has no account, and collects no analytics or telemetry.
